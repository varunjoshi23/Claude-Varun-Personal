      Method: Pages were fetched with the site's robots.txt crawl delay of 10 seconds respected, then rendered in Chromium. Client testimonials and quoted court opinions were not grammar-checked. Readability: Flesch reading ease and sentence length are measured on each page's unique paragraphs. "Boilerplate share" is the proportion of paragraph text that repeats on 8 or more of the 15 pages.
