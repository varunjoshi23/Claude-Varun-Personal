# Central Arkansas Service Company (centralarservice.com): Content Inconsistency Review

**Pages reviewed (15):** Home, About, Team, Contact, Services, Service Areas, Financing, Plans, Careers, Recent Projects, Air Conditioning, Heating, Plumbing, Water Heater, HVAC Maintenance
**Not reviewed:** the other 656 URLs in the sitemap (city pages, service subpages, blog, learning center)
**Date:** 9 November 2026
**Method:** I read the raw HTML of each page (exact characters) and re-checked the key findings in a rendered browser. Every quote below is copied from the live page.

---

## 1. How the company names itself

The company name appears in four different forms in page titles, and one title has no brand at all.

| Version | Where |
|---|---|
| `... \| CASC` | 12 of 15 page titles |
| `Plumbing Services in Central Arkansas \| Central AR Service` | /plumbing |
| `Family Owned & Local \| Central Arkansas Service Company` | /team |
| `Water Heater Services Central Arkansas Repair & Installation` (no brand, and "in" is missing) | /water-heater |

In body text the name is always "Central Arkansas Service Company" or "CASC", which is consistent. "Central AR Service Co" appears only inside a customer review and should stay as written.

**To decide:** one title suffix for every page. "| CASC" is already used on most of them.

## 2. The ownership tagline

The site's main tagline is **"Locally owned. Family operated."** (About H1, footer, every call-to-action band). Other pages say it differently:

| Wording | Where |
|---|---|
| "Locally owned. Family operated." / "locally owned and family operated" | About, footer, CTA bands, Careers |
| "Family Owned & Local" | /team title and eyebrow |
| "a family-owned and locally operated business" (the two halves are swapped) | /hvac-maintenance |
| "this family-owned and operated company" | /heating |
| "a truly local, family-owned alternative" | /plumbing |

The /hvac-maintenance version is the one that really clashes, because it turns the slogan around.

## 3. Why the company was founded

All pages agree on the date (September 25, 2024, or "September 2024" on the service pages) and on the founders (Jamie Wilson, Owner; Brandon Wilson, President). The reason for starting the company is told differently:

| Wording | Where |
|---|---|
| "bought up by large outside investors" | About, Team, Heating, Water Heater |
| "an industry increasingly dominated by large, out-of-state private equity investors" | Air Conditioning |
| "large, investor-owned service giants" | Plumbing |
| "the decisions get made here in North Little Rock, not in another state" | Home |

"Private equity" and "out-of-state" are more specific claims than the version on the About page. Either back them up on About, or bring the service pages in line with About.

## 4. Team size and fleet: only on service pages, written five ways

| Wording | Where |
|---|---|
| "our 11-person team runs 9 clean, branded trucks" | Air Conditioning |
| "our 11-person team runs 9 branded service trucks" | Heating |
| "We are an 11-person team running nine clean, branded trucks" | Plumbing |
| "our eleven-person team" | HVAC Maintenance |
| "a locally owned, family-operated team of eleven, running nine clean, branded trucks" | Water Heater |
| *(not mentioned)* | Home, About, Team, Careers |

The number itself agrees everywhere (11 people, 9 trucks). The formatting doesn't, and the figure is missing from the pages where a visitor would look for it (About and Team). A headcount repeated across five pages will go out of date at different times on each one.

**To decide:** confirm the current figures, then either state them once on About and Team or remove them from the service pages.

## 5. Opening hours

| Wording | Where |
|---|---|
| "Mon-Sun: 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM" | Header, footer and CTA band on every page |
| "Mon–Sun 7–6" | Home hero stats |
| "7-6" / "Hours, Mon-Sun" | About, Team |
| "Monday through Sunday from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM" | Plumbing |
| "Monday through Sunday from seven in the morning until six in the evening" | HVAC Maintenance |
| "Closed Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day." | Contact only |
| "We'll contact you within 1 hour during regular business hours" | Contact (meta and body) |

Two content gaps, beyond the formatting:
- The holiday closures appear only on Contact. Every other page says Mon-Sun with no exceptions, and pricing mentions a $149 "weekend & holiday" call.
- "Regular business hours" is never defined. Visitors can't tell whether it means 7–6 daily or something narrower.

## 6. Plans page: what's on offer doesn't match how it's described

- The section heading says **"Three plans"**, but the third card, Water Heater Flush ($159 "per flush"), is described on the same card as a "Separate maintenance service". It is a one-off service, not a plan.
- The meta description says **"Two seasonal visits a year and 10% off qualifying repairs"** for the residential and commercial plans together. The Commercial HVAC Maintenance Program is "Quarterly visits".
- In "Covers gas furnace, AC, heat pumps, air handlers, and mini splits", one item is singular and the rest are plural. The same card spells the term two ways: "mini splits" and "mini-split head".

The prices themselves are consistent sitewide: residential plan $189 for the first system and $99 for each additional one, flush $159, 10% member discount, $99 weekday and $149 weekend/holiday service calls.

## 10. Inconsistent house style

| Convention | What the site does now |
|---|---|
| Numbers | Mostly numerals ("11-person", "50-mile", "10-year"). HVAC Maintenance writes them as words ("eleven-person", "fifty-mile", "ten-year parts and two-year labor warranty"). |
| Contractions | Mostly avoided ("do not", "is not"). Used on Heating ("isn't", "aren't", "Don't", "won't"), Plumbing ("won't", "isn't"), HVAC Maintenance ("haven't") and Contact ("We'll"). |
| Dashes | Em dashes only on Heating and Water Heater ("inefficient—it can", "tactics—just"). |
| Oxford comma | Roughly half and half: 39 with, 31 without. Home and Team mostly without; Heating, Plans and Water Heater with. About uses both. |
| Hyphenation | "short-cycling" and "short cycling" both appear on the Heating page. |
| Dates | "September 25, 2024 in North Little Rock" (Home) has no comma after the year; About has "September 25, 2024, in North Little Rock". |

Spelling is US English throughout, with no UK forms.

## Other issues found during the review (outside consistency, but visible to customers)

1. **The WordPress default "Sample Page" is live and in sitemap.xml** (/sample-page). It carries the site's header and footer, says "The XYZ Doohickey Company was founded in 1971", and its meta description contains a raw `<p>` tag.
2. **Template bug that doubles category names** in body links and section headings:
   - Plumbing: "Plumbing Plumbing Repair" (4 times), plus "Plumbing Garbage Disposals", "Plumbing Sump and Sewage Ejector Pumps", "Plumbing Gas Line Installation" and "Plumbing Backflow Testing and Repair" as headings.
   - Heating: "Heating Heating Repair" (2 times), plus "Heating Furnace Replacement", "Heating Heat Pump Repair" and similar.
   - HVAC Maintenance: "a HVAC Maintenance Commercial HVAC Maintenance Program", "HVAC Maintenance Residential HVAC Maintenance Plan".

   It probably comes from one CMS link template, so the 656 pages not reviewed are likely affected too.
3. **The Recent Projects page never loads its feed.** After a full browser render it still shows "Loading recent projects. If nothing appears, call 501-307-1310…".
